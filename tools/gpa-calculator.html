    About these calculations

    • Formula: GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) ÷ Σ(credits). In percentage mode, the weighted average uses scores instead of grade points.
    • Rounding: results are rounded to 2 decimals (half up).
    • Letter equivalent: the highest grade on your scale at or below your GPA. Honors, dean’s list and probation thresholds differ by school, so check yours.
    • Scales differ. The built-in presets use the most common US conversions. For other systems (for example 4.3, 4.5, 5.0 or a 10-point scale), choose “Custom”, enter your grades and points (up to 10), then enter grades as letters or grade points.
    • Retakes: exclude the old attempt. If a retake is already included in your previous GPA, follow your school’s rules to adjust that number first.
    • Privacy: your entries are saved only in this browser (on your device) so you do not have to retype them. “Reset all” erases them.
    • This tool gives an estimate. Your official GPA is the one issued by your school.
