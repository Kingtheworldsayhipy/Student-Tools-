    Power (P)V × II² × RV² ÷ R
    Voltage (V)I × RP ÷ I√(P × R)

    How it was calculated

      Steps are shown in base units (V, A, W, VA, var).

      Formulas

      S = V × I
      Apparent power, single-phase (VA)
      P = V × I × PF
      Real power, single-phase (W)
      S = √3 × V × I
      Apparent power, three-phase (V = line-to-line)
      P = √3 × V × I × PF
      Real power, three-phase
      Q = S × √(1 − PF²)
      Reactive power (var), same as √(S² − P²)
      PF = P ÷ S = cos φ
      Power factor and phase angle φ

      Three-phase results assume a balanced load; V is the line-to-line voltage and I is the line current. The sign of Q (lagging or leading) is not shown.

        A month is taken as 30 days and a year as 365 days.

        Formulas

        E = P × t
        Energy = power × time (kWh when P is in kW and t in hours)
        Cost = E × price
        Price is per kWh
        1 kWh = 3.6 MJ
