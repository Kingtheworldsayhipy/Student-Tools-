    Good to know

    • Gallons, quarts and fluid ounces here are US units. The imperial gallon is listed separately.
    • Calories are thermochemical (1 cal = 4.184 J). A food "Calorie" is 1 kcal.
    • Pound (lb) is the avoirdupois pound; lbf is pound-force.
    • A year is 365.25 days. Data sizes: kB, MB, GB use powers of 1000; KiB, MiB, GiB use powers of 1024.
    • Temperature uses formulas, not a single factor (it has an offset).
